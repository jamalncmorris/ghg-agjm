NOTES:
A NOTE ON SCOTLAND: Looking for a rep in Scotland? There are few, if any reps in Scotland as solicitors have until very recently not attended police stations there. The law was not interpreted in the same way as in England and Wales.
 
A NOTE ON CUSTODY TELEPHONE NUMBERS: The Custody Telephone Numbers are as accurate as we can get them. We apologize if there are any mistakes in our database. If you find an error please let us know. Click here to contact us. Please email us any numbers which are missing from the database.
 
A NOTES ON DISTANCES: The distance calculations are pretty rough and are a guide only. They are a rough estimate of the distance between two postcodes as the crow flies. The calculation makes no allowance for roads or traffic.
 
ANOTHER NOTE ON DISTANCES: We are grateful for the work of Paul Jenkins who did the maths. If postcode data was available at a reasonable cost our distance calculations would be more accurate. But the postcode database is not free in the UK. Sadly it is too expensive for a small non-profit site like this. The Post Office sells their database. In most countries it is provided free of charge or at a minimal cost. For more information a good place to start is by clicking here to read a relatively recent Guardian article.
 
YET ANOTHER NOTE ON DISTANCES: If you get a result in excess of 3000 miles then please let us know. Whilst it should never happen errors are inevitable. To contact us please click here.
